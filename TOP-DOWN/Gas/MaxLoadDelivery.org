#+title: Max Load Delivery

* Maximal load delivery (MLD) problem

_task_: determine feasible steady-state operating points for joint gas-power networks while ensuring the maximal delivery of gas and power loads simultaneously

*the MLD problem maximizes the amount of nongeneration gas load and active power laod simultaneously*
- nongeneration gas load: gas demand uncommitted to electric power generators)

Objective term relating to the amount of nongeneration gas load be defined by:

$\eta_G (d) := (\sum\limits_{i \in D^{'}}\beta_i d_i) (\sum\limits_{i\in D^{'}}\beta_i \bar{d}_i)^{-1}$
- where $\mathcal{D}^{'}$ is the set of the delivery points in the gas network not connected to interdependent generators in the power network
  - $\beta_i \in \mathbb{R}_+$ is a predefined restoration priority for delivery $i \in \mathcal{D}^{'}, d_i$
    - $d_i$ is the variable mass flow of gas delivered at $i \in \mathcal{D}^{'}$ and $\bar{d_i}$ is the maximum deliverable gas load at $i \in \mathcal{D}^{'}$

Objective term relating to the amount of active power load can be defined by

$\eta P(z^d) := (\sum\limits_{i\in \mathcal{L}} \beta_i z^d_i |\mathbb{R}(S^d_i)|)(\sum\limits_{i\in \mathcal{L}} \beta_i |\mathbb{R}(S^d_i)|)^{-1}$

- $\mathcal{L}$ is the set of loads in the power network
- $\beta_i \in \mathbb{R}_+$ is the load restoration priority for load $i \in \mathcal{L}$
- $z_i \in |0,1|$ is a variable that scales the absolute maximum amount of active power load
  - $|\mathbb{R}(S^d_i)|$ , at load $i \in \mathcal{L}$
