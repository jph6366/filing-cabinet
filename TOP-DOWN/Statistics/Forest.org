* Random Forests ~ Leo Breiman(2001)

https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~breiman/randomforest2001.pdf

_Random forests_ are a combination of tree predictors such that each tree depends on the values of a random vector  sampled independently and with the same distribution for all trees in the forest.


- the _generalization error_ for forests converges a.s. to a limit
  - as the number of trees in the forest becomes large
- the _generalization error_ of a forest of tree classifiers
  - depends on the strength of the individual trees in the forest
    - and the correlation between them
