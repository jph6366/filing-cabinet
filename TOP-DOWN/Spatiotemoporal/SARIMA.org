#+title: Seasonal Arima


* Seasonal ARIMA


** Definition

$ARIMA(p,d,q)$ model is a non-seasonal forecasting model
- to incoroporate seasonality we need seasonal terms
  - whose order is indicated by capital letters $(P,D,Q)$

*Seasonal ARMA model* data shows seasonal pattern every $s$ observations
- but after taking seasonal difference
  - the data behaves like ARMA model (stationary)

*Seasonal ARIMA model* data shows seasonal pattern every $s$ observations
- but after taking seasonal difference
  - the dat behaves like ARIMA model (non-stationary)
